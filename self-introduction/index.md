**Hello,**

My name is Xenia, but I prefer to go by Aksee, and Im 24 years old. Im a junior front-end developer, and today I d like to share a bit about myself and my journey into programming.

I originally graduated with a degree in humanities, but I quickly realized that I didnt want to dedicate my life to something I wasnt truly passionate about. So, I took a gap year to search for something that would excite me every day, something I could fully commit myself to.

One day, while joking about IT, it suddenly clicked! I had always been drawn to technology. Looking back, everything I truly enjoyed was somehow related to IT: my love for math and structured thinking, creativity from design and art school, and problem-solving and teamwork from organizing group activities. Even my passion for video games and tech-related articles pointed in the same direction.

After doing extensive research on different IT fields, everything pointed to front-end development as the perfect fit for me. It combines logic with creativity, structured thinking. To build my skills, Ive taken multiple courses on Udemy, Yandex, tons of YouTube or free internet tutorials, and eventually joined RS School. RS School is truly unique - the community, the approach, and the results stand out. It starts off easy but quickly ramps up in difficulty. The quality of tasks and the level of support are on another level. Every assignment taught me something new or helped me sharpen my skills.

I know I cant learn everything at once since there s so much to grasp, but that s what I love about this field it constantly challenges me and keeps me engaged. Im really excited about the prospect of working in a dedicated team where communication and collaboration play a huge role in achieving common goals.

Right now, my tech stack includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and TypeScript. I feel comfortable using them and always strive to deepen my understanding.

I have some expirience in working on a real project as a volonteer, i took role of a feature lead. It gave me better udnerstanding what actual work looks like from inside, from people communication to backend. I got to know the microservice applciation on Docker, Mongo, Redis & Postman, i improved my GIT skill, teamwork, component writing on React. I was also helping and giving small bug tasks for people who just joined.

My current goal is to strengthen my problem-solving skills, dive deeper into JavaScript and TypeScript, and gain real-world experience by building projects. I want to become a strong front-end developer, contribute to a team, and create meaningful user experiences.

Thats all for now. Thank you for your time! Im excited to be part of this journey at RS School.
